Why it's accurate

This isn't a keyword search

1

Counts jobs, not listings

One job posted in five cities is one job. Counting it five times is the fastest way to look unserious to anyone who knows how hiring works.

2

Caps every signal

A company with forty open sales seats doesn't beat a company building its first go-to-market team. Volume isn't the same as intent.

3

Shows its proof

Every score opens into the real job postings behind it. You can check the number instead of taking our word for it.

4

Admits what it missed

If a job board can't be read, it's reported as a failure — never quietly counted as a zero to make the results look cleaner.

What it can't do

Honest limits

  • It reads job titles, not full descriptions. A posting that mentions Clay or Apollo in the body is a strong signal this version doesn't catch yet.
  • It only sees public job boards. A company hiring quietly is invisible to it.
  • Hiring isn't the same as buying. This tells you who to call first. It doesn't close anyone for you.
  • This list is a demonstration. Pointing it at your actual target market is a change to one file.
